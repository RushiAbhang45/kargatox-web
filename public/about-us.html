ABOUT KARGATOX

About Kargatox: Research & Marketing Agency in Pune

Kargatox is a founder-led research and marketing agency based in Pimple Saudagar, Pune. We help founders and leadership teams across India understand their market, decode their customers and turn that insight into campaigns that move revenue.

Knowledge partners, not vendors: we work inside your team and stay close enough to the data to know it is working.

WHO WE ARE

Built on research. Measured in growth.

Most businesses don't have a marketing problem. They have an information problem. They spend on ads, content and campaigns without a clear picture of who their market is, why customers buy, or which channel is actually bringing in revenue.

Kargatox was started to close that gap. We bring market research, consumer behaviour analysis and campaign analytics together with SEO, social media marketing and brand campaign strategy under one roof, so the insight and the execution never drift apart.

Every engagement is scoped against a number that needs to move: pipeline, retention or revenue. You always know what we are doing, why we are doing it and what it is meant to change for your business.
